Exact slice coefficients · why crossings open into gaps 


        

          FiniteFree constructs the exact multivariate determinant, renames its
          context with substitute, and calls
          restrict_line([0,y,z],[1,0,0]) at every rational
          control-grid point. The univariate
          derivative(monic=False) is checked against the restricted
          exact multivariate partial derivative. Embedded strings preserve exact
          coefficients. Browser curves, Jacobi eigenvalues and bracketed
          derivative roots use Float64; the displayed derivative curve is
          divided by 4 for scale.
        

        

          At z = 0, roots are −y times the four diagonal entries, so the colored
          tracks show those diagonal identities and cross. At z > 0, the real
          symmetric tridiagonal pencil is irreducible, giving simple
          eigenvalues; colors then follow ascending root ranks. Rolle's theorem
          gives weak derivative interlacing, with equality at repeated roots.
          These facts concern this identity-direction pencil; the plots do not
          certify general hyperbolicity or stability. Regenerate with
          PYTHONPATH=. python examples/interactive_geometry.py.
