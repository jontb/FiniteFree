Coupling, interlacing & exact coefficients 


        

          At z = 0, p(s) = (s² − y²)(s² − 9y²). At y = z = 0 this becomes s⁴,
          with derivative 4s³. For z > 0 the symmetric tridiagonal pencil is
          irreducible and has simple spectrum. Rolle's theorem gives r₁ ≤ d₁ ≤
          r₂ ≤ d₂ ≤ r₃ ≤ d₃ ≤ r₄ for the sorted polynomial and derivative roots;
          the inequalities are strict for distinct roots.
        

        

          FiniteFree constructs the exact pencil determinant and calls
          restrict_line([0,y,z],[1,0,0]) on the rational control
          grid. Univariate derivatives are checked against restricted
          multivariate partials. Embedded coefficients are exact; browser
          eigenvalues, root positions and residuals use Float64 and supply no
          general hyperbolicity or stability certificate. Regenerate with
          PYTHONPATH=. python examples/interactive_geometry.py.
