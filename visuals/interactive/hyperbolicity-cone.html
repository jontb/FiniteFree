          Why this is a spectrahedron · exact data & numerical limits
        
        

          The pencil at e = (1,0,0,0) is the identity. Its closed hyperbolicity
          cone is the PSD component; t = 1 cuts out the 3 × 3 correlation-matrix
          spectrahedron. Here PSD is equivalent to all three displayed 2 × 2
          principal minors and the determinant being nonnegative (the 1 × 1
          minors equal 1). On the slice, the boundary satisfies x² + y² − 2zxy =
          1 − z². At |z| = 1 the slice collapses to a line segment; at |z| >
          1 it contains no PSD point.
        

        

          FiniteFree constructs the exact determinant and substitutes t = 1
          before exporting rational sparse coefficients. Surface meshing,
          polynomial evaluation, eigenvalues and membership labels in this
          offline browser use Float64, with finite resolution and tolerances.
          This display does not certify general hyperbolicity or stability.
          Regenerate with
          PYTHONPATH=. python examples/interactive_geometry.py.
