        Homogeneous cone & exact model
        

          For M(t,x,y,z) = tI + M(x,y,z) − I, the determinant is P(t,x,y,z) = t³
          + 2xyz − t(x² + y² + z²). Its closed hyperbolicity cone about e =
          (1,0,0,0) is {M(t,x,y,z) ⪰ 0}; t = 1 cuts this four-dimensional cone
          in the correlation-matrix spectrahedron shown here. The fixed-z
          boundary satisfies x² + y² − 2zxy = 1 − z². Its PSD portion
          degenerates to a segment at |z| = 1 and is empty for |z| > 1. In
          the slice, purple det > 0 regions have two negative eigenvalues;
          gray det < 0 regions have one.
        

        

          FiniteFree exports exact rational determinant coefficients after
          substituting t = 1. Browser meshing, eigenvalues and membership use
          Float64 and scaled tolerances; the display supplies no general
          hyperbolicity or stability certificate. Regenerate with
          PYTHONPATH=. python examples/interactive_geometry.py.
